## What's new in Pane 1.1

- **Teleprompter.** Paste your script and read it from a strip at the top of the screen,
  next to the camera. It follows your voice as you talk, so there's nothing to scroll,
  and it's never in the recording. Lines in `[brackets]` are reminders, shown but not read.
- **Retakes cut for you.** Say a sentence again and Review cuts the first try, with a
  short pause before the retake and soft joins. Keep or cut each one in the timeline.
  Sent to Final Cut, kept retakes go on a layer of their own.
- **A folder for every video.** Give a recording a name (or start your script with
  `# Title`), and its edited copy, captions and Final Cut files all go in one folder.
- **Recording Management** (⇧⌘L) lists every video with its size: review, rename, move
  to the Trash, or keep only the edited copy.
- **Blurs keep up with fast scrolling.** A blur now moves with the page instead of
  stretching into a tall patch while you scroll, and text scrolling back into view is
  covered from the moment it shows.
- **Safer updates and files.** Updates must come with a signed feed, quitting during a
  recording saves it first, and an interrupted export never leaves a half-written copy.
